Metode SMART (Simple Multi-Attribute Rating Technique)

Mengkonversi nilai mentah menjadi nilai utility berskala 0 - 100 berdasarkan ekstremum kriteria. Benefit Utility: u = 100 x (Cout - Cmin) / (Cmax - Cmin). Cost Utility: u = 100 x (Cmax - Cout) / (Cmax - Cmin).
